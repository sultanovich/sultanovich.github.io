The **DebConf26 Local Team** has been working for a long time on the key steps required to prepare the next **[DebConf26](https://debconf26.debconf.org/)**, which will take place in Santa Fe, Argentina.

After confirming that the venue for the event will be the **[FICH](https://www.fich.unl.edu.ar/) (Faculty of Engineering and Water Sciences)**, the next major milestone was the **Site Visit**—a crucial stage in the preparation of every DebConf.

During this visit, members of the **DebConf Committee** review the venue, evaluate the technical infrastructure (networking, audio and video), inspect lodging and transport options, and assess the capacity of the **Local Team** to host an event of this scale.

In August 2025, **Stefano Rivera** and **Nattie Mayer-Hutchings** visited Santa Fe to carry out this inspection. They toured the FICH facilities, checked technical aspects, and took the opportunity to meet with the **DebConf Local Team**.

Although the visit was meant to be a simple technical inspection, it evolved into something much more meaningful: an encounter among people and communities who share Debian’s values of collaboration, transparency, and collective building.

Alongside the Site Visit, several talks and activities were organized:

A summary of this visit and the related activities was also published on **[Debian Bits](https://bits.debian.org/2025/09/bits-from-argentina-august-2025.html)**, the official Debian blog, highlighting the energy and commitment of the Argentine community on the road to **[DebConf26](https://debconf26.debconf.org/)**.
